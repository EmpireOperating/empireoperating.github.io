Privacy notice

Effective October 8, 2026

This notice covers AO ECA Maintenance, Empire Operating’s private, operator-controlled Google maintenance tool, and its informational pages. Google access requires the operator’s consent; publishing this notice does not activate access.

Overview Privacy notice Usage terms

Google data and permissions

The tool requests only these four Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly — read Drive file metadata, such as identity, type, and edit capabilities; not broad Drive file contents.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — read, edit, create, and delete Google Sheets spreadsheets.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.projects — read Apps Script source and create or update projects.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.deployments — read and maintain Apps Script deployments.

These permissions are account-wide, subject to the consenting account’s access rights. OAuth does not technically restrict them to one application. Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, and broad Drive content, write, copying, or sharing scopes are not requested.

Purpose and task limits

Google data is used to review, diagnose, and carry out specifically approved spreadsheet and Apps Script maintenance. Initial API acceptance checks are limited to account identity, project/source/deployment information, workbook metadata, and a designated sheet’s header row—not member data rows or legacy cells.

A separately authorized dashboard inspection may display personal information. Further data access and any production or QA change, deployment, deletion, or permission change require explicit approval for the exact task. Google consent alone is not approval for those actions.

Local storage and cloud AI

The tool runs on an operator-controlled computer. Its separate OAuth client and grant are kept in a local, owner-only credential directory. Credentials, authorization codes, and token-bearing callback URLs must not be placed in chats, public pages, or task records. This notice does not claim that local storage is encrypted at rest.

AO is configured to use an external AI service from OpenAI. Maintenance requests, source excerpts, API results, and other information included in agent context may be processed by that service. Local credential storage does not mean all maintenance information remains on the computer.

Google user data is not sold by Empire Operating or used for advertising. Training general-purpose AI models is not a permitted use. Before Google user data is included in external AI processing, the operator must confirm that the provider and selected settings comply with these restrictions and Google’s Limited Use requirements; otherwise that data must be kept out of external AI processing. This notice does not claim that a provider’s retention or training protections have already been verified.

Empire Operating’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Human access is limited to the operator and explicitly authorized maintenance, security, or legally required purposes.

Retention and deletion

Local source snapshots, maintenance conversations, API acceptance results, and evidence may be retained to support continuity and auditability. There is no fixed automatic deletion schedule established for these records. The operator manages their retention and removal.

External AI services may apply their own retention rules. Provider-side deletion, backup removal, and deletion of every historical copy are not guaranteed by deleting a local file. Contact Empire Operating to request a review of retained information or its deletion; any legal, security, or backup limitations will be explained rather than promising a deadline that is not established.

Revoke Google access

The operator can review and revoke this tool’s access through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future access through that grant; it does not automatically delete previously retained records or information processed by an AI service. Keep unrelated Google authorizations intact.

These informational pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and load Google Fonts. Hosting and font providers receive ordinary web-request information when the pages are visited. The pages contain no sign-in flow, forms, or analytics scripts and do not receive Google authorization tokens. Those statements describe these maintenance pages, not every other page on this domain.

Questions and requests

Contact Empire Operating at empireoperating@proton.me. Do not include passwords, client secrets, tokens, authorization codes, or unnecessary personal records. This notice must be updated before materially different data practices are introduced.
