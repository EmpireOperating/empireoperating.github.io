Usage terms

Effective October 8, 2026

AO Maintenance is an operator-only tool run by Empire Operating. These terms describe its operating boundaries; it is not a public service or a sign-up offer.

Overview Privacy notice Usage terms

Operator authority

Only the designated operator may authorize this tool. The operator must have permission to access the relevant Google account and maintain the systems involved. Visiting these pages does not grant access to a spreadsheet, Apps Script project, dashboard, or private application.

Google consent is not task approval

The operator personally completes Google sign-in, multifactor authentication, and Google consent using a separate installed Desktop OAuth client and local loopback callback. Existing authorizations must be preserved; credentials and browser sessions must not be copied from another device or agent.

The four requested scopes provide account-wide capabilities for Drive metadata, Google Sheets, and Apps Script projects and deployments. They are not technically confined to one business system. Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, and broad Drive read/write/copy/sharing scopes are outside this grant.

Every production or QA change, deployment, deletion, permission change, and allowlist change requires separate operator approval for the exact task. Consent to a Google permission does not authorize those actions, and one task’s approval does not carry over to another.

Maintenance safeguards

Review must precede live changes. Begin with the minimum information necessary, such as metadata, source and deployment checks, before requesting additional records. Read-only inspection must not submit forms, save edits, or weaken access controls without separate authorization.

Missing API access, account settings, unexpected project or deployment identities, and permission drift are reasons to stop and request a specific decision—not permission to change settings silently. OAuth or website configuration is not approval to alter application access controls.

AI assistance and records

AO uses AI assistance, including a configured external OpenAI service. AI output can be incomplete or wrong; it is not evidence that a change is safe or that a deployment succeeded. Live actions require scope checks, appropriate testing, and readback of the affected system.

Cloud AI processing, local records, retention limits, and deletion requests are described in the privacy notice. Google data must be kept out of external AI processing until the provider and settings are confirmed to comply with the notice’s data-use restrictions.

Security and stopping access

Do not put passwords, client secrets, authorization codes, access tokens, token-bearing callback URLs, or private personal records into these pages or support messages. If credential exposure or an unexpected access condition is suspected, pause the affected workflow and have the operator approve the appropriate recovery action.

The operator may stop using the tool and revoke its Google access. Revocation does not automatically delete retained local or provider records and must not revoke unrelated grants. No uninterrupted availability, permanent authorization, or automatic backup/deletion guarantee is offered.
